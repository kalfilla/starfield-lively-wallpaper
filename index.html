About & shortcuts

Quick controls and share-link keys for the screensaver.

FToggle fullscreen.
Esc / F11Exit fullscreen.
SpacePause or resume.
↑ ↓Increase or decrease speed.
← →Adjust roll.
RReset defaults.
CToggle clock.
MCycle straight, pointer, and auto-drift steering.
PSave the current view as a PNG.

Click the canvas to enter fullscreen, then click it again to exit. Pointer steering follows mouse or touch; the link button copies your settings. Auto fullscreen exits when activity resumes. A delay of 0 disables it.

Share URL keys

Put ? before the first setting and join more with &, for example ?hm=1&af=1&afd=30&afc=1. For Hide mouse, hm=1 hides the cursor and hm=0 keeps it visible.
